# Keraton Galuh (region `keraton_galuh`, area prefix `kg_`), Act II (MQG, story v4c)

Built from `zones/mataram.md`'s format. Look: a Sundanese royal capital of stone terraces and walls, a big square with a ring, banyan trees, loosely based on the Karang Kamulyan site in Ciamis (`VERIFY`). Placeholder tiles from the tile table in [tatar_sunda.md](tatar_sunda.md) (`#` wall, `.` grass, `,` path, `~` water, `b` banyan (bamboo tile), `r` rock, `_` stone floor).

Story v4/v4b/v4c ([quests.md](../quests.md) section 4, MQG g.1-g.9, [story.md](../story.md) "Pacing model (v4c)" and "Depth pass (v4b)"): this region is Act II, "The Borrowed Face", in three chapters (Tournament Day, The Wagon in the Yard, The Borrowed Face). Step numbers below are the v4c ones; every `Play` piece has a place ("Piece placement per step"). New entity `clue.tscn` (`res://entities/world/clue.tscn`, params `{clue_id, memory_id?, cond?}`). The Kelir memories and the Laras chapter of this act are their own scenes under `zones/kelir/` ("Kelir memory scenes" below; `## Memory:` headings, not generated by `gen_zones.py`). `kg_pamali` (the Act III stone, `ford` and `truce` beats and the duel) belongs to MQ2; its markers are the MQ2 rows below. World order: Desa Purwa, Keraton Galuh, Pamali, Mataram. Kabut flags: `mqg_3` (palace gate), `mqg_6` (portrait gallery and throne room), `mqg_7` (Pamali gate), `lamps_3` (inner garden, the g.3 lamp puzzle). Encounter flags: `mqg_1`, `mqg_2`, `mqg_h3` (guards `galuh_hall`). New flag ids `mqg_a1`, `mqg_h1`-`mqg_h3`, `mqg_lc`. **Side stories v4d additions (2026-11-06):** SQ-G1 (Si Kabayan, `kabayan`, `galuh_official`, clues `g1_decree` and `g1_basket`, memory `sq_g1_kabayan`) and SQ-C1 (clues `c1_feathers` and `c1_basket`, memory `sq_c1_basket`, the rooster find) are in kg_alun; SQ-G2 (`kaputren_maid`, clues `g2_broom` and `g2_roll`, memory `sq_g2_servant`, the write card `ririwa_name`, the elite `ririwa` in the Kaputren corridors) and SQ-C2's `galuh_queen` at the cage are in kg_keraton; the camp talk `camp_mataram` (Hariang Banga) is in kg_pamali. Three memories are added to "Kelir memory scenes". The new legend chars are new spots on grass or floor; the map dimensions are unchanged.

## Area 1: kg_alun

- Size: 60 x 34 tiles = 2 x 2 screens (30 x 17 each).
- Look: Town square of the false king's capital: grass plaza, banyan corners, a paved tournament ring in the centre (a stone terrace, loosely Karang Kamulyan, VERIFY). West road to Desa Purwa, north steps to the palace, east road to the Pamali.
- Purpose: Ch 1 and Ch 2 hub (step g.1 arrival: the crowd, the rooster seller, the plan with Ciung; g.2 the bouts, the champion; g.3 Lengser). Tournament rounds `galuh_bout_1` then `galuh_bout_2` in the ring, Lengser appears after round 2, gates to the palace (kabut `mqg_3`) and the Pamali (kabut `mqg_7`).

No roaming enemies; the arena is the throne room. No arena posts (Barma Wijaya has none in the brief; add `arena_post.tscn` rows if his script wants them).
