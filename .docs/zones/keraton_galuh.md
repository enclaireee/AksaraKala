# Keraton Galuh (region `keraton_galuh`, area prefix `kg_`), Act II (MQG, Story v5)

Built from `zones/mataram.md`'s format. Look: a Sundanese royal capital of stone terraces and walls, a big square with a ring, banyan trees, loosely based on the Karang Kamulyan site in Ciamis (`VERIFY`). Placeholder tiles from the tile table in [tatar_sunda.md](tatar_sunda.md) (`#` wall, `.` grass, `,` path, `~` water, `b` banyan (bamboo tile), `r` rock, `_` stone floor).

**Story v5 pass (2026-11-06, ASSUMED until playtest).** Steps, flags and conds follow [quests.md](../quests.md) section 4 MQG g.1-g.20 (v5, 0-based `step:` indices where used) and the v5 script. Step flags: g.1 `mqg_a1` then `mqg_0`; g.2 `mqg_p1`; g.3 `mqg_p2`; g.4 `mqg_1`, `mqg_2`; g.5 `mqg_3`, `mqg_4`; g.6 `mqg_ar1`; g.7 `mqg_ar2`; g.8 `mqg_kp1`; g.9 `mqg_kp2`; g.10 `mqg_kp3`; g.11 `mqg_4a`; g.12 `mqg_4b`; g.13 `mqg_5`; g.14 `mqg_lc`; g.15 `mqg_6` (**Choice 2**, `choice_freed` / `choice_handed`); g.16 `mqg_6b`; g.17 `mqg_h1`, `mqg_h2`; g.18 `mqg_h3`; g.19 `mqg_6c`; g.20 `mqg_7`. The v4c flag meanings are gone: `mqg_6` no longer opens the gallery or throne room (the gallery door `W` is `mqg_6b`, the throne door `K` is `mqg_h2`), `mqg_4` is the petilasan (g.5), and the Pamali truce is **Choice 3** (`choice_mercy` / `choice_judgment`; v4's `choice_silent` is retired). Laras flags in conds: `flag:laras_key_archive_grate`, `flag:laras_sabotage_glamour_seal` (set by LC2, g.14). NPC ids chosen: the Kaputren maid is `g2_maid` (quests.md section 5 and the v5 side script; `kaputren_maid` and the earlier duplicate spots in `kg_keraton` are dropped); the Pamali dalang stays `ki_sumarto` here (the script's staging-figure id, same character as `mataram_dalang` in Mataram). New ASSUMED clues `kaputren_mirror` (g.8) and `wagon_ruts` (g.11); the `nyai_sekar` and `pasar_informant` NPC ids are the legend's own. Story v4/v4b/v4c ([quests.md](../quests.md) section 4, MQG g.1-g.9, [story.md](../story.md) "Pacing model (v4c)" and "Depth pass (v4b)"): this region is Act II, "The Borrowed Face", in three chapters (Tournament Day, The Wagon in the Yard, The Borrowed Face). Step numbers below are the v4c ones; every `Play` piece has a place ("Piece placement per step"). New entity `clue.tscn` (`res://entities/world/clue.tscn`, params `{clue_id, memory_id?, cond?}`). The Kelir memories and the Laras chapter of this act are their own scenes under `zones/kelir/` ("Kelir memory scenes" below; `## Memory:` headings, not generated by `gen_zones.py`). `kg_pamali` (the Act III stone, `ford` and `truce` beats and the duel) belongs to MQ2; its markers are the MQ2 rows below. World order: Desa Purwa, Keraton Galuh, Pamali, Mataram. Kabut flags (v5): `mqg_3` (palace gate), `mqg_6b` (portrait gallery door), `mqg_h2` (throne room door), `mqg_7` (Pamali gate), `lamps_3` (inner garden, the g.3 lamp puzzle). Encounter conds: `mqg_p1`/`mqg_p2` (riggers), `mqg_p2` then `mqg_1`/`mqg_2` (bouts), `mqg_kp1` (courtyard), `mqg_kp3` to `mqg_4a`/`mqg_4b`/`mqg_5` (cell yard), `mqg_h3` (guards `galuh_hall`, throne approach). New flag ids `mqg_a1`, `mqg_h1`-`mqg_h3`, `mqg_lc`. **Side stories v4d additions (2026-11-06):** SQ-G1 (Si Kabayan, `kabayan`, `galuh_official`, clues `g1_decree` and `g1_basket`, memory `sq_g1_kabayan`) and SQ-C1 (clues `c1_feathers` and `c1_basket`, memory `sq_c1_basket`, the rooster find) are in kg_alun; SQ-G2 (`kaputren_maid`, clues `g2_broom` and `g2_roll`, memory `sq_g2_servant`, the write card `ririwa_name`, the elite `ririwa` in the Kaputren corridors) and SQ-C2's `galuh_queen` at the cage are in kg_keraton; the camp talk `camp_mataram` (Hariang Banga) is in kg_pamali. Three memories are added to "Kelir memory scenes". The new legend chars are new spots on grass or floor; the map dimensions are unchanged.

- Purpose: Ch 1 and Ch 2 hub (step g.1 arrival: the crowd, the rooster seller, the plan with Ciung; g.2 the bouts, the champion; g.3 Lengser). Tournament rounds `galuh_bout_1` then `galuh_bout_2` in the ring, Lengser appears after round 2, gates to the palace (kabut `mqg_3`) and the Pamali (kabut `mqg_7`).
