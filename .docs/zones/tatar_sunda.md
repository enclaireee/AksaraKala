# Tatar Sunda (region `tatar_sunda`, 8 areas), Act 1

Zone design on paper. Step 1 of the world design workflow ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) section 5). Sources: [world.md](../world.md), [bestiary.md](../bestiary.md) (roster, Encounters, Glyph weaknesses), [quests.md](../quests.md) (MQ0 0.9, MQ1 1.1-1.15, SQ-S1 to SQ-S5, marker rules), [story.md](../story.md) "Pacing model (v4c)", "Depth pass (v4b)" and "Hunts (v4)", [architecture.md](../architecture.md) 9b (Clue, Kelir memory). **Story v4/v4b/v4c pass (2026-11-06):** step numbers below are the v4c ones (15 steps, chapters Tea and the Trail, Sandekala, The Batu Tulis and the Weaver, The Lake and the Crater). Every `Play` piece of 1.1-1.15 has a place (see "Piece placement per step"); new entity `clue.tscn` (`res://entities/world/clue.tscn`, params `{clue_id, memory_id?, cond?}`); the Kelir memories and Laras chapters of this act are their own scenes under `zones/kelir/` (section "Kelir memory scenes" below, not generated by `gen_zones.py`: they use `## Memory:` headings). A builder turns each area section into one scene without redesign: change the layout here first.

Tatar Sunda is no longer one 120 x 51 zone. It is a region of 8 interlocking areas (scenes), each 60 x 34 tiles = 2 x 2 screens (30 x 17 each). Coordinates are (x, y) from the top-left tile (0,0) of that area. Area ids use the prefix `ts_`. The links between areas are topological, not a literal screen grid. The old single-zone id `tatar_sunda` stays only as the region name.

Design goals (Bloodborne-style): loops that come back on themselves, shortcuts that open from the far side, one one-way drop, and a boss landmark that is seen from the first area. Tone stays warm and forgiving: no enemies in the village, no fight between the last petilasan and the boss, shortcuts only save time.

Build rule: a char may repeat only when scene and props are identical (2-tile exit, 2-tile kabut, 2-tile shortcut gate). Every other char is one spot with the floor tile listed under it (paint the floor first, then place the spot).

Spawn ids follow `from_<other area id>`. A spawn is 1 tile, 1 to 2 tiles inside its exit. Exits sit on the map edge, 2 tiles wide.

## Loops
- **L1 fence loop**: ts_kebun -> ts_kampung -> (G) ts_leuweung -> grove east end -> south path -> south corridor -> `sc:ts_pagar` pocket -> ts_kebun. Opens the first shortcut.
- **L2 ladder loop**: ts_kampung -> (H) ts_sawah -> ts_situ -> `sc:ts_tangga` -> ts_kampung. Skips the whole sawah climb on every later trip.
- **L3 crater loop**: ts_kampung -> ts_sawah -> ts_situ -> (K) ts_kawah_jalan -> drop -> ts_kampung. After the boss, or a death run past the crater, the drop returns the player to the village in seconds.
- **L4 gorge loop** (new, Pacing 5): ts_kampung -> ts_leuwi passage -> hamlet -> sande room -> `sc:ts_leuwi` tunnel (opens from the sande room) -> passage -> ts_kampung. After Sandekala the way back is 15 tiles, not 70.

Branches off loop nodes: ts_leuwi (the whole Hunt K1 and Sandekala, steps 1.3-1.6, a branch off ts_kampung that is now loop L4); optional areas only: the dark grove, hidden spring and alcove off ts_leuweung (L1); the Kujang room off ts_situ (L2/L3).

## Critical path
ts_kebun (arrival slice: pickers and the grandson, `sunda_picket`, ribbon, E1 `sunda_edge`, P1, Cepot) -> ts_kampung (ambush `sunda_kampung` with its elite boar, Abah Karna, Ga) -> ts_leuwi (the hamlet at dusk and the K1 hook, three clues, memory `k1_maghrib`, Sandekala; back by the `sc:ts_leuwi` tunnel) -> `G` -> ts_leuweung (lane E2, three name-stones, grove E3 `sunda_batutulis`, batu tulis Nga) -> back to ts_kampung (by the lane, or the south path (40..41,20..30) and south corridor to the pagar pocket, never through the dark grove, which opens `sc:ts_pagar`) -> `H` -> ts_sawah (E4 clearing, E5 terraces with its elite, weaver and the Boeh Rarang, coops and loom) -> ts_situ (the dam puzzle, felled trees, `sunda_lakeshore` with its elite, the toy, Naganingrum and Ca) -> `K` -> ts_kawah_jalan (E7 crater road, P2, voice and LC1) -> ts_kawah (Captain, the `crater_seal` beat, mask fragment and H1, boat ribs and K2, Sangkuriang) -> return: P2 -> drop -> ts_kampung -> ts_kebun -> Desa Purwa. No glyph is needed as a key: kabut open on quest flags set by talking and reading, never by casting; the two puzzles use interact only. Glyphs are rewards.

Off the path: the dark grove elite, the hidden spring, the alcove, the Kujang room, the sawah and crater-road chests, 5 angklung, the codex (ruin, maghrib custom, Crater Seal).

## Landmark
The Tangkuban Perahu silhouette (the overturned-boat mountain). It is a backdrop art layer, not a map tile. It is visible in the sky of ts_kebun (small, far north) and grows through ts_sawah, ts_situ and ts_kawah_jalan until it fills ts_kawah, where the boss waits at its foot. The player sees the goal from the first screen and finds the way to it by loops. ASSUMED, art layer, one sprite scaled per area.

## Area 1: ts_kebun

- Size: 60 x 34 tiles = 2 x 2 screens (30 x 17 each).
- Look: Open tea terraces and a bamboo fence, afternoon light. The Tangkuban Perahu silhouette fills the north sky.

Holds the gate of shortcut `sc:ts_pagar`.
