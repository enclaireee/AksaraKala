# Audio

Music, ambience and sound effects for Aksara Kala. **Every sound is generated by `python3 tools/gen_audio.py`** (numpy + ffmpeg, no samples, no downloads, no licences) into `assets/audio/` as Ogg Vorbis. Related: [architecture.md](architecture.md) 3 (Audio autoload), [combat.md](combat.md) (hit feedback), [ui.md](ui.md), [assets.md](assets.md) 4.1.

Status (2026-11-09): **first full pass, tuned by measurement only (levels, spectra, loop seams, register checks), not yet by ear and not yet run in Godot.** Listen to every track, then tune with the knobs in section 7. Tags: ASSUMED (a decision), VERIFY (a culture fact), TUNE (a number to adjust by ear).

---

## 1. Design goals

1. **It must read as an RPG soundtrack, not a loop pack.** Every region has an exploration theme, a grim variant, a combat theme and a boss theme built from the *same* melody, so the score has identity per place and the game has leitmotifs.
2. **Gamelan and degung are the voice.** Real instrument models (saron, demung, peking, bonang, gender, kenong, kempul, gong ageng, kendang, bedug, suling, rebab, tarompet, kacapi, angklung) over the colotomic structure (gong cycle, kenong, kempul, kethuk) with a Western RPG arrangement on top (pads, bass ostinato, drum fills, risers).
3. **The soundtrack reacts.** Combat is a layer, not a cut. Bosses take over. Stingers duck the mix. Menus and casting muffle it.
4. **Time is the motif.** `The Script of Time`: a faint clock tick runs under the title theme, the Gua Kala themes and ambience, and the Batara Kala boss.

## 3. Ambience (`assets/audio/amb/`, seamless stereo beds, 24 s)

`forest_day`, `forest_night`, `village`, `market`, `river`, `sea`, `cave`, `crater`, `desert`, `swamp`, `ruins`, `graveyard`, `kala`, `palace`, `prison`, `kelir`, `forge`. Chosen per zone from `Audio.AMBIENCE` (all 47 zones listed; the test fails if one is missing). They play on the Ambience bus under the music, crossfade 2.5 s, and dip when a boss starts.

## 4. Sound effects (`assets/audio/sfx/`, mono, peak-normalised per category)

- **Player:** `swing_1..3`, `swing_heavy`, `hit_light/heavy/kill/armor/flesh`, `player_hurt`, `dodge`, `perfect_dodge`, `shoot`, `arrow_hit`, `block`, `flask`, `stamina_empty`, `player_death`, `step_<grass|stone|sand|wood|water>_<0..2>` (surface from `Audio.SURFACE*`).
- **Aksara casting:** `cast_begin` (time slows, world muffles), `ink` (looping stylus-on-bark while drawing, pitch follows speed), `cast_fail`, `cast_ok_low/mid/high` (by score), `cast_perfect` (score >= 0.92), and one spell sound per glyph: `spell_bolt` (Sunda Ka), `ward` (Ga), `bind` (Nga), `burst` (Ca), `heal` (Jawa Ha), `dash` (Na), `rally` (Ca), `summon` (Ra, tiger), `ombak` (Ka).
- **Enemies and bosses:** `enemy_death`, `stagger`, `enemy_telegraph`, `boss_telegraph`, `boss_slam`, `boss_roar`, `boss_phase`, `boss_death`.
- **World:** `chest_open`, `pickup`, `pickup_rare`, `exit`, `petilasan`, `lontar` (clue shimmer), `thud`, `shortcut_open`, `puzzle_solve`, `kabut`, `memory_in/out`, `save`, `wrong`, `torch`.
- **UI:** `ui_move`, `ui_confirm`, `ui_back`, `ui_denied`, `ui_open/close/page` (the field book: lontar leaf turn), `ui_slider`, `ui_letter`, `ui_choice`, `ui_equip`, `ui_pip`, `ui_start`.
- **Dialogue:** `blip_0..5`, soft bamboo knocks; the speaker key's hash picks the pitch so each character has a recognisable voice (narration is quieter).

## 5. How the game plays it

`autoload/audio.gd` (Audio) + `components/snd.gd` (`Snd`, the static front door: `Snd.play("hit_light")`, `Snd.music("title")`, `Snd.jingle(...)`, no-op when the autoload is missing).

- **Buses:** Master > Music, SFX, Ambience. Created on start; volumes come from `user://settings.cfg` (`vol_music`, `vol_sfx`, `vol_ambience`); the Sistem tab has a slider for each (`UI_SYS_AMBIENCE`). Music and Ambience carry a low-pass filter that closes while the menu is open (1.7 kHz) or while casting (1.1 kHz).
- **Zones:** `Events.zone_entered(id)` -> `plan_for(id)` -> base track (+ combat + boss + ambience + footstep surface). Unknown ids (test zones) change nothing. The same base id never restarts.
- **Combat layer:** polled 4 times a second. An enemy within 230 px that is chasing, winding up, attacking, recovering or staggering (EnemyBase `State` 1-6), or any enemy within 90 px, starts combat; it lingers 4 s. The combat layer fades in over about a second and the base ducks 13 dB (synced) or 40 dB (towns and dark zones, where the combat track starts from 0).
- **Bosses:** a node in group `boss` with `engaged` and `health.hp > 0` (the HUD's rule) -> `boss_roar` + `boss_intro` stinger + the region's boss theme; base and combat go silent but keep playing, so the zone music resumes mid-phrase afterwards. A phase change plays `boss_phase`; Batara Kala phase 3 switches to `boss_kala_2`. `Events.boss_defeated` plays `boss_death` + `victory` and holds the music silent for 9 s, then the zone fades back.
- **Death:** `player_died` plays `player_death` + the `death` stinger and holds the music for 7 s; the respawn's `zone_entered` brings the zone back.
- **Memories:** `memory_started` -> `memory_in` (reversed gong), `kelir` (or `ruwatan`) music and ambience; `memory_done` -> `memory_out`.
- **Scenes:** title, prologue/epilogue, name entry and class select call `Snd.music(...)`; the next zone takes the music back.
- **Story cues:** a dialogue line `#music:<id>`, `#music:` (silence), `#sfx:<id>`, `#amb:<id>`, `#jingle:<id>` is handled by Audio (scene_cue), so scripts can score a beat without code.
- **UI sounds are automatic:** Audio hooks `SceneTree.node_added`; every `BaseButton` gets move (focus/hover) and confirm (pressed) sounds, every `Slider` a tick. Explicit hooks: field book open/close/page turn (`menu.gd`), dialogue blips (`dialogue_box.gd`), area card stinger (`area_title.gd`).
- **Hooks in gameplay code** (all `Snd.play`, one line each): `player.gd` (swing per combo hit, dodge, perfect dodge, hurt, shoot, flask, hit light/heavy/kill, blocked -> stamina_empty/wrong, Footsteps child), `enemy_base.gd` (stagger), `boss_kit.gd` (telegraphs, slam when shake >= 3 px, roar), `chest.gd`, `shortcut.gd`, `zones.gd` (exit), `cast_overlay.gd` (cast begin/end, ink, fail). Events handled in Audio: `spell_cast` (spell sound + quality chime), `enemy_killed`, `item_picked`, `clue_examined`, `puzzle_solved`, `trial_completed`, `petilasan_activated`, `rested`, `quest_started/updated/completed`, `glyph_learned`, `area_cleared`, `class_chosen`, `name_written`, `dialogue_*`, `Game.saved`.

## 6. The generator (`tools/gen_audio.py`, `tools/audio/`)

`dsp.py` (filters, reverb, mixer, master), `inst.py` (instrument models), `theory.py` (scales, note strings), `music.py` (arrangement engine), `tracks.py` (themes and the explore / dark / combat / boss / town / special arrangements), `sfx.py` (effects and stingers), `amb.py` (ambience beds).

- `python3 tools/gen_audio.py` builds everything (about 4 minutes on 4 cores, 22 MB). `--only music/boss`, `--only sfx/`, `--list`, `--jobs N`.
- **Deterministic:** every file reseeds from its name, so a rebuild reproduces the same bytes' worth of sound.
- **Composition format:** a theme is `bal` (balungan: 8 bars x 4 one-beat tokens) + `lead` (8 bars x 8 half-beat tokens), digits are scale degrees, `'` up an octave, `,` down, `.` rest, `-` hold. Bars 9-16 repeat 1-8 shifted by `shiftB` scale steps with the last bar replaced (`endB_*`). Chord roots come from each bar's first balungan note.
- **Registers:** `Theme.f(degree, octave, lo)` clamps an instrument to its real range (saron >= 260 Hz, bonang >= 330, gong >= 55, bass >= 45, suling >= 330, rebab >= 200 ...). Do not add instruments without a `lo`.
- Output levels: music RMS about -19 dBFS (combat -17, boss -16, limiter ceiling -2.2 dBFS so Vorbis cannot clip), ambience -27, jingles -3 dB peak, sfx peak -1.5 to -16 dB by category.
- Checks used while building: loudness, spectral balance (5 bands), loop-seam jump vs normal sample steps, register table, and a spectrogram view. None of these replace listening.

## 7. Tuning by ear (first things to check)

1. **Mix balance per layer:** gains are the `gain=` arguments in `tracks.py` arrangements (explore / combat / boss). Combat should be clearly louder and denser than explore at the crossfade; if the swap feels abrupt raise explore's duck (`Audio._mix`, -13 dB).
2. **Melodies:** the 16 balungan/lead strings in `tracks.py` are the tune. Edit them like sheet music, re-run `gen_audio.py --only music/<id>`.
3. **Brightness:** `music.eq` (+8 dB above 1.8 kHz) and the reverb IR damping in `dsp.make_ir`.
4. **Footstep rate:** `Footsteps.STRIDE` (13 px). **Combat radius/hold:** `Audio.COMBAT_RANGE`, `COMBAT_HOLD`.
5. **Per-sound volumes:** the peak dB column in `gen_audio.py` `SFX` (and `vol_db` at the call site).
6. **Dialogue blips** may feel busy at `CPS`: change the `vis % 2` rate in `dialogue_box.gd`.

## 8. Known gaps (next pass)

- Not run in Godot: `--import` first (it creates the `.import` files and the `Snd` class cache), then `tests/audio_test.gd`, `menu_test`, `ui_test`, `loop_test`. Then play and listen: every region, one fight, one boss, a memory, the menu, casting.
- No positional audio: all sfx are non-spatial (the camera is top-down and the view is small).
- No per-enemy voices or attack sounds beyond hit/stagger/death/boss telegraphs; add per-kind sounds in `enemy_base.gd` subclasses when the bestiary pass starts.
- Weather, day/night and time-of-day ambience swaps are not modelled.
- Real recorded instruments (kendang, demung, suling, sinden vocals) would lift the score further; the generator is the stand-in and the file ids and the Audio code do not change if a recording replaces a file.
- **VERIFY:** that the tunings and the pairing of scales to regions (degung for Sunda, pelog for the courts, slendro for Mataram, a harder pelog for the east) are acceptable to practitioners; the compositions are original and not transcriptions of any traditional piece.
