# Story

All legends and details are summarized from common retellings. They were fact-checked once (Nov 2026), against secondary sources only (Wikipedia, news, travel sites). Primary texts (the Pararaton and others) and expert review are still open. Items still marked **VERIFY** were not confirmed. Sources are in [assets.md](assets.md).

**Story v5 major RPG expansion & anti-repetition pass (user call 2026-11-06: "for other bosses and other characters don't make it too repetitive, make it a much longer game, and put more depth into each part of the story, each character, each boss, each region").**
- **Scale:** Expanded from a ~9.5 h main story (78 steps, 40 areas) to a **~21.5 h main story (138 steps, 1,295 min modeled, 49 areas across 6 regions + hub; ~35 h+ at 100%)**, with **4–5 full chapters per region**.
- **Anti-repetition:** No two Acts or bosses use the same formula. The v4 "3 Clues -> 1 Kelir Memory -> Fight" Hunt loop is now **only one of several regional structures** (used where a folklore mystery fits, such as Sandekala). Other Acts and bosses use **environmental progression & rising floodwaters** (Sangkuriang), **undercover palace intrigue & faction heist** (Barma Wijaya in Galuh), **two-front war campaign & real-time night-building siege** (Bandung Bondowoso & Hariang Banga in Mataram), **stalking predator ambushes across multiple areas + 7-generation blood-curse mystery** (Buto Ijo, Keris Mpu Gandring & Aria Kebonan in Tanah Timur), **village recovery & three sacred sea trials** (Segara Kidul), and **three-realm memory descent + dual-perspective boss finale** (Gua Kala).
- **Cross-time Laras chapters (`LC1`–`LC6`):** Expanded into deeper playable segments where Laras's actions in captivity (**hiding keys, shifting mechanisms, carving true-name wards `laras_key:*`, and sabotaging Seals during her forced readings `laras_sabotage:*`**) directly open routes, shortcuts, and boss vulnerability phases for the hero in the present, culminating in a **dual-perspective final battle** against Batara Kala.
- **Deeper dialogue & cast:** Lifted the strict 4-line cap to **8–12 lines for main-path and camp talks** (and **8–10 lines for story beats**), added optional **"Ask about..."** dialogue branches (`"quiet": true` topic loops) on major NPCs and companions, and expanded the regional cast budget to **4–5 named characters per region** with multi-act arcs.
- **Six Major Choices (one per Act):** Choices now ripple across regions—changing which allies fight beside you, unlocking secret routes, modifying boss phases, and shaping the playable Epilogue.

This file is the canon. [quests.md](quests.md) 4 has the step tables, [script.md](script.md) has every line, and the zone docs place everything.

## Spine: "Aksara yang Terlupa: Gerhana Kala & Jejak Laras" (The Forgotten Script: The Eclipse of Kala & Laras's Trail)
In this world, magic is written in *aksara*, and a written name anchors a soul in the world. What nobody writes down, Batara Kala eats.

Desa Purwa is the old Geger Sunten, where an old couple (Aki and Nini Balangantrang in the legend; in play "the old couple next door") raised a foundling boy, **Ciung Wanara**. The false king of Galuh wants that boy dead. At the village *wayang ruwatan* the dalang reads the children's names aloud to ward them, Ciung's among them (**VERIFY** the name-reading in a ruwatan). The **Nameless Hunter** (*Si Tanpa Ngaran*) hears names spoken aloud, and that night he and his **Faceless** (*Wong Tanpa Rupa*) raid the village for the boy. Ciung has already run west. In the crowd, your younger sister **Laras** (`La-Ra-Sa`) looks at the masked Hunter and whispers "Aria", a name nobody has said to him in fifteen years. Laras can hear True Names. The Hunter takes her instead: "Wrong child. A better one." In the struggle her palm-leaf *lontar* tears in two. You keep the half with her name on it; she keeps the half with yours.

By morning nobody in Desa Purwa remembers Laras, not even Ibu. Only you, Semar and your half of the *lontar* still remember. Whatever Laras writes on her half shows faintly on yours. She keeps writing *Ka*, for *kakak*, so she won't lose you, and while she writes it you can't forget her. Her letters fading is the clock. You are a player-named hero; your lines use `@NPC_HERO_NAME`. Across six regions (49 areas) you follow Laras's trail, forge alliances with rival princes and forgotten guardians, and confront the ancient curses woken in her wake. Meanwhile the Hunter uses her gift, and the war between Galuh and Mataram, to break the **Four Great Seals** and wake the **Eclipse of Batara Kala**.

## The Four Great Seals
The ancestors wrote four dangerous stories down so they would stay *finished*. A written ending binds its curse. Those four writings are the Great Seals, and together they keep Kala asleep under the land.
- A seal can only be **unread** by someone who hears True Names. The Faceless have no names, so they can't read it. That is why the Hunter needs Laras.
- When a seal breaks, its legend **replays** across the entire region (rivers dammed and rising, temples rising in the night, seven generations of blood-curse marks burning on doors) and Kala eats what people forget.
- A reading **cracks** a seal. After that any hand can break it—**but because Laras fights back during every forced reading (`LC1`, `LC3`, `LC4`), she plants a hidden flaw (`laras_sabotage:*`) in each seal**, giving the hero the opening needed to end the legend again.
- A reading can't be taken back. Each one is a step of Kala's waking.
- The hero never breaks a seal. You **end the story again**, the way the legend ended. That stops the rampage in the region, though it doesn't undo the reading. Seal 2 is the exception: it was a *name*, and a name can be written again. Whatever Kala has already eaten stays eaten until Kala is calmed in the finale.

| # | Seal | Who breaks it, where | Laras's sabotage (`LC`) | How you end it again |
|---|------|----------------------|-------------------------|----------------------|
| 1 | The Crater (Sangkuriang) | Laras is forced to read it at Tangkuban Perahu; the Faceless Captain smashes the cracked stone (Act I) | In `LC1`, Laras slips a river stone into the seal's dawn-groove (`laras_sabotage:crater_seal`) and marks the eastern post (`laras_key:crater_post`) | The false dawn: hang the *Boeh Rarang* on the eastern post Laras marked and cast Sunda `Ca` |
| 2 | The Royal Name of Galuh | Prabu Barma Wijaya himself, fifteen years ago. He gave Kala his own name and scraped King Nagawiru's name off the Batu Tulis | In `LC2`, Laras steals the royal signet wax in the Kaputren (`laras_sabotage:glamour_seal`) and opens the archive grate (`laras_key:archive_grate`) | Unmask Barma Wijaya in Act II, then write Nagawiru's true name (`Na-Ga`) on the Pamali stone in Act III |
| 3 | The Thousand Temples (Bandung Bondowoso) | Laras is forced to read it at Candi Seribu under threat to the prisoners (Act III) | In `LC3`, Laras skips the thousandth verse and hides the master chisel under the central altar (`laras_sabotage:bondowoso_pillar`, `laras_key:candi_crypt`) | Sound the lesung women's false-dawn rhythm and cast Jawa `Ca` to shatter his unanchored pillars |
| 4 | The Keris (Mpu Gandring) | Laras is forced to read it at the Eastern Shrine, and the Hunter draws the blade (Act IV) | In `LC4`, Laras binds her own blood-ink *Ka* onto the seventh curse stake (`laras_sabotage:keris_seventh_stake`) and opens the buried wind-tunnel (`laras_key:sand_tunnel`) | Cleanse the seven curse stakes with Wirasana's technique, lay the keris to rest, and decide its fate (Choice 4) |

When the fourth seal opens, the Eclipse begins. The Eclipse eats first the name of whoever read the seals open. The Hunter believed it would eat whoever *commanded* the reading—him.

**Two kinds of forgetting.** The Faceless are forgotten people. Their names are bound in the lontar bundle at the Hunter's belt, and they obey whoever holds them. Names in the bundle are *held*; names Kala ate are *gone*. When the Hunter falls, the bundle opens and every held name flies home, all but Laras's, which the Eclipse eats first. What Kala ate comes back only when Kala is calmed.

Codex: the seals are a game invention built on the real legends. The codex marks each line "legend" or "game".

## Laras's Cross-Time Trail & Playable Chapters (`LC1`–`LC6`)
Laras is never a passive captive. She observes the enemy from the inside, speaks with prisoners and the Faceless Deserter, and actively sabotages the Hunter's operations so you can succeed behind her.

Each act has:
1. **Physical traces & cross-time keys (`laras_key:<id>`):** In her playable chapter (`LC1`–`LC6`), Laras unlocks a grate, shifts a sluice counterweight, carves a true-name ward on a hidden door, or aligns a tidal echo. When you return to hero control, **that physical change persists in the zone**, unlocking a route, puzzle solution, or shortcut.
2. **Seal & Boss sabotage (`laras_sabotage:<id>`):** During her forced seal readings, Laras alters a stroke or hides a ritual component. In the boss fight that follows, her sabotage is what makes the boss's shield or phase mechanic vulnerable to your glyphs.
3. **Playable mechanics in `LC1`–`LC6`:** Shown in the cool-blue Kelir memory frame (`#D5E2F0` lit, `#1E2A3A` dim). Laras cannot fight with weapons; she uses stealth, environmental interactions (keys, levers, wards), dialogue with captives, and her gift **"Say the True Name"** (freezes a Faceless patrol for 3 s as they remember who they were). Each chapter lasts **5–8 minutes** (counted as `chapter, puzzle` in the pacing model) and ends with her writing *Ka* on her half of the lontar.

## Pacing & Dialogue Rules (Story v5)
The story plays like a full-scale narrative RPG (inspired by *The Witcher 3*, *Bloodborne*'s interconnected world, and *FromSoftware*'s boss-as-character stakes).

### Dialogue & Storytelling Rules (TUNE)
1. **Rich conversations, zero lore-dumps:**
   - **Main-path & camp talks:** Up to **8–12 lines** per conversation (lifted from v4's 4-line cap), with natural back-and-forth between `{name}`, companions, and NPCs.
   - **Optional `"Ask about..."` hub topics:** Major NPCs and companions at petilasans/camps offer 2–3 optional `"quiet": true` dialogue choices (e.g., *"Ask about King Nagawiru"*, *"Ask about Laras"*, *"Ask about the war"*), letting players dig into character motives and regional life without bloating the critical path.
   - **Story beats:** Up to **8–10 lines** for major dramatic turns, revelations, and boss confrontations. Encyclopedic cultural history still lives in the Codex (1–3 sentences per entry).
2. **Know before you meet (Rule 10):** No character, boss, or cultural term is named on the main path before the player has seen it or had it naturally contextualized in the same breath. Checked automatically by `python3 .docs/know_check.py`.
3. **Name tags on first speech (Rule 11):** First time any named character or boss speaks, their one-line `_WHO` subtitle appears under their name ([ui.md](ui.md) 3).
4. **Bosses are characters first:** Every boss is seen, heard, or felt in the region *before* you step into their arena—whether stalking you across three zones (Buto Ijo), debating you in court (Barma Wijaya), dueling you out of filial duty (Hariang Banga), or reshaping the map around you (Sangkuriang's dam, Bondowoso's rising pillars).
5. **The fight is taught through play (Rule 13):** Every boss's core mechanic is learned organically before the fight—sometimes through a **Kelir memory** (`K1`, `K2`, `K3`, `K5`, `K7`), sometimes through **multi-zone survival encounters** (Buto Ijo's pouches across Tanah Timur), **palace faction sabotage** (Laras's `LC2` signet wax against Barma Wijaya), or **combat sparring** (Jawa `Na` guard-break before Hariang Banga).
6. **Story so far & Grouped Clues (Rule 14):** Every completed main step adds a 1–2 line past-tense recap (`Q_<QUEST>_S<n>_DONE`) to the Journal's **"Story so far"** tab, and clues are grouped by investigation/arc.

### Pacing Model Piece Types (`pacing_model.py`, TUNE)
Each main step in [quests.md](quests.md) §4 lists its gameplay pieces in the `Play` column. Because v5 features deeper conversations, larger multi-encounter areas, and 5–8 minute cross-time Laras chapters, the model checks:
- **Piece weights:** `talk` (1.0 min), `beat` (1.5 min), `lesson` (1.5 min), `write` (1.5 min), `clue` (2.0 min), `fight` (2.0 min), `travel` (4.0 min), `puzzle` (4.0 min), `memory` (4.0 min), `chapter` (6.0 min, Laras cross-time segment), `miniboss` (4.0 min), `boss` (6.0 min), `setpiece` (5.0 min).
- **Rules enforced by `python3 .docs/pacing_model.py`:**
  1. **No thin steps:** Every main step is at least **5.0 minutes** of combined play (Interlude steps at least 3.0 min).
  2. **Play vs. Reading balance:** At least **55% hands-on play** (`fight`, `travel`, `puzzle`, `miniboss`, `boss`, `setpiece`, `chapter`, `memory`) and at most **25% reading** (`talk`, `beat`, `lesson`) in every combat Act.
  3. **Rhythm:** A story piece (`clue`, `talk`, `beat`, `memory`, `chapter`, `write`) at least every **12 minutes**; a major payoff (`miniboss`, `boss`, `setpiece`, `beat`) at least every **28 minutes**; never more than **3 reading pieces** in a row.
  4. **Side-quest bands:** **S** (8–15 min), **M** (15–28 min), **L** (25–45 min).

## Detailed Act-by-Act Breakdown (Story v5, 138 Steps)

### Prologue: Desa Purwa — "The Last Ordinary Day" (MQ0, 12 steps, 3 chapters, ~80 min)
Regional character: **Ibu**.
- **Ch 1: Morning and Market (0.1–0.4).** Laras wakes you and dares you to a game of tag across Desa Purwa (`0.1`, teaches movement, dodge, and shows her gift as she calls a stray cat and the old banyan by their true names). Share morning tea with Ibu—two cups set out (`0.2`)—and help the old couple next door fetch wild **Ciung Wanara** and his strange egg down from the banyan ridge (`0.3`). Walk the morning market (`0.4`), where the stall-keeper says a tall stranger in a blank mask has been asking children's names. Cepot meets you at the sanggar door and hurries you inside.
- **Ch 2: The Sanggar and the Ruwatan Night (0.5–0.8).** Visit **Semar** and **Cepot** at the sanggar (`0.5`); Semar says a written name keeps a person in the world, and Laras writes her name and yours at opposite ends of her palm-leaf lontar (`0.6`). Help light the ceremonial lamps around the wayang stage as dusk settles (`0.7`), then play **Semar's wayang ruwatan (`K0`)** behind the lit kelir (`0.8`), learning how Batara Kala devours the unwarded and how the ancestors sealed four dangerous legends in writing.
- **Ch 3: The Morning After (0.9–0.12).** During the name-reading, shadows step off the screen: the Faceless raid the village (`0.9`). Ciung flees west; Laras whispers *"Aria"* to the masked Hunter, who seizes her instead (*"Wrong child. A better one"*), tearing the lontar in half. Wake to a village that has forgotten her: Ibu sets out two cups without knowing why, and you pull Laras's half-lontar from the ashes (`0.10`). Semar remembers, teaches you Sunda `Ka` (*Bolt*, for *kakak*) in the sanggar (`0.11`), and helps you drive the returning Faceless vanguard from the square before confronting the Hunter's shadow at the west gate (`0.12`).

### Act I: Tatar Sunda — "The Crater Seal" (MQ1, 24 steps, 5 chapters, ~210 min)
Regional character: **Dewi Naganingrum**. Ally: **Ciung Wanara**. Glyphs: Sunda `Ga` (*Ward*), `Nga` (*Bind*), `Ca` (*Burst*).
- **Ch 1: Tea and the Bamboo Trail (1.1–1.5).** Enter the dawn tea terraces (`ts_kebun`), meet the elder's grandson playing his angklung, and break the Faceless ambush at the bamboo edge where Laras tore free and left her ribbon (`1.1`). Track her footprints through the mist-shrouded tea slopes (`1.2`), meet Cepot at the first petilasan (`1.3`), defend `ts_kampung` against a Faceless & wild boar raiding party (`1.4`), and earn Sunda `Ga` (*Ward*) from the kampung elder (`1.5`), who saw Laras fleeing toward the river gorge with "a wild village boy."
- **Ch 2: The Dusk Spirit of the Gorge — Hunt K1 (1.6–1.10).** Reach the gorge-side hamlet at sunset (`1.6`), where a weeping mother calls her missing child's name into the bamboo dark. Investigate the hamlet's unlit maghrib *oncor* torches, the boy's sandal by the rapids, and the split tracks of Laras and Ciung (`1.7`–`1.8`). Experience **`K1` ("A Child at Maghrib")** (`1.9`) to learn how **Sandekala** hunts in unlit shadow, then defeat Sandekala in the darkening gorge by lighting the four *oncor* (`1.10`), rescuing the hamlet's children and finding Laras's trail climbing toward the ancient inscription stone.
- **Ch 3: The Defaced Stone and the Terraces (1.11–1.15).** Climb through `ts_leuweung` and solve the three moss-covered name-stones (`1.11`) to reach the **Batu Tulis**, where King Nagawiru's name was gouged out fifteen years ago and Laras carved her warning underneath: *"They need me to read"* (`1.12`, earning Sunda `Nga` *Bind*). Descend to `ts_sawah`, where Sangkuriang's waking curse is already damming the river and flooding the lower paddies (`1.13`). Help the Weaver defend her terrace loom from a Jurig Leuweung pack (`1.14`) and repair the upper sluice gates to receive the sacred **Boeh Rarang** cloth (`1.15`).
- **Ch 4: The Exiled Queen by the Lake (1.16–1.19).** Follow the rising floodwaters through `ts_leuwi` (`1.16`), discovering felled giant timber and empty rooster coops (`K2` clues, `1.17`). Clear the lakeshore of `ts_situ` and recover a fallen Faceless soldier's carved wooden toy (`1.18`, proving the masks are stolen villagers). Meet **Dewi Naganingrum** in exile by the lake (`1.19`): she recognizes Ciung Wanara's description as her lost son, reveals the royal conspiracy, and teaches you Sunda `Ca` (*Burst*).
- **Ch 5: The Rising Waters and the Crater Seal (1.20–1.24).** Ascend `ts_kawah_jalan` through the Captain's rear guard to the upper petilasan (`1.20`). Play **Laras Chapter `LC1` ("Reading Slowly")** (`1.21`): as Laras on the crater rim, freeze patrolling guards with their true names, wedge a river stone into the Crater Seal's dawn-groove (`laras_sabotage:crater_seal`), and mark the eastern post (`laras_key:crater_post`). Storm the rim, defeat the **Faceless Captain** (`1.22`), and witness Hunter memory **`H1` ("The Kind King")**. As the dying Captain shatters the cracked seal, experience **`K2` ("Dayang Sumbi's Night")** (`1.23`), then defeat **Sangkuriang** (`1.24`) using Laras's marked eastern post, the *Boeh Rarang*, and Sunda `Ca`. Free Ciung Wanara and the naga-king **Nagawiru**, and make **Choice 1** (`choice_tell_ciung` vs. `choice_keep_secret`).

### Act II: Keraton Galuh — "The Borrowed Face" (MQG, 20 steps, 4 chapters, ~175 min)

Solve the archive lamp-and-mirror cipher to reveal the scraped royal genealogy (`g.8`). Cross into the **Kaputren (`kg_kaputren`, royal women's quarters)** (`g.9`), evading royal sentries to inspect the chambers where Laras was held and where Queen Pangrenyep's attendants whisper of a king who never looks in a mirror (`g.10`).

In his confession, Barma Wijaya reveals **Seal 2**: he is the servant Aria Kebonan, and the Nameless Hunter is the true name he fed to Kala. Regional characters: **Ki Sumarto** and **Nyai Gendhis**. Rival/Ally: **Hariang Banga**. Glyphs: Jawa `Ha` (*Heal*), `Na` (*Dash-strike*), `Ca` (*Rally*), `Ra` (*Summon*).

Break the siege on the foothill shrine (`2.11`) to earn Jawa `Ra` (*Summon*) from the juru kunci (`2.12`). Smash through the newly risen spirit-pillars across the plain (`2.13`), storm the Faceless reading-camp at `mt_candi` (`2.14`), and recover Laras's *Ka*-covered lontar strip alongside the chisel-marked rubble (`2.15`).

- **Ch 5: Blood on the Pamali Ford (`kg_pamali`, 2.20–2.24).** Take the temple shortcut back toward the Pamali river (`2.20`), fighting through vanguard skirmishes to reach the sacred **Pamali Stone** and write King Nagawiru's true name (`Na-Ga`, restoring Seal 2, `2.21`). Watch Ki Sumarto's shadow play **`K-III` ("The Brothers Who Split the Land")** at the river camp (`2.22`), read Hariang Banga's unsent letters to his caged mother (`2.23`), and face **Prince Hariang Banga** in an honourable 1v1 duel in the river shallows (`2.24`). When he yields, Nagawiru's voice rises from the river and Ciung appears on the western bank: brothers do not shed brother's blood (*pamali*). Make **Choice 3** (`choice_mercy` vs. `choice_judgment`).

As you cross `tt_kulon`, **Buto Ijo crashes through the savanna in Stalking Ambush #1** (`3.2`)—cursed mad by the waking fourth seal, his hide is impervious until you drive him back to the canyon. Survive **Buto Ijo Stalking Ambush #2** at the dry riverbed (`tt_kali`, `3.6`) by testing the first bramble-seed ward. Consecrate the remaining pouches at the ancient banyan shrine in `tt_beringin` while fending off **Stalking Ambush #3** (`3.7`), experience **`K4` ("Timun Mas's Flight")** (`3.8`), and lure **Buto Ijo** into his final lair where all four pouch fields break his rampage (`3.9`).
- **Ch 3: The Seven-Generation Blood Curse (`tt_beringin` & `tt_ladang`, 3.10–3.13).** Return to find Mpu Gandring's waking curse igniting stakes across `tt_ladang` and the forge quarter (`3.10`). - **Ch 5: The Eastern Shrine and Aria's Face (`tt_candi`, 3.18–3.22).** Use Laras's wind-tunnel shortcut to breach the outer shrine (`3.18`), where you finally **see Laras** across the altar as she is forced to finish reading Seal 4. ## Sensitivity
- **Cockfighting** is in the Ciung Wanara legend. The hero never bets or handles a rooster, and the bouts happen off-screen. The codex notes the legend and states the game does not endorse cockfighting (**VERIFY** legal wording before shipping).
- **Sunda and Java:** The legend's message is that brothers do not fight (*pamali*). Neither side is depicted as evil.
- **Kala and ruwatan:** Batara Kala is calmed and turned back through *ruwatan*, never killed. The codex explains *ruwatan* as a living tradition.
- **Nyi Roro Kidul:** Presented as belief, with respect, and is never fought or touched.
- **Living rituals:** Yadnya Kasada (Tengger) and *sedekah laut* / *labuhan* (south coast) are treated with respect as cultural/community backdrop, never turned into monster fights.
