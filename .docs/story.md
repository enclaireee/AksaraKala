## Spine: "Aksara yang Terlupa: Gerhana Kala & Jejak Laras" (The Forgotten Script: The Eclipse of Kala & Laras's Trail)
In this world, magic is written in *aksara*, and a written name anchors a soul in the world. What nobody writes down, Batara Kala eats.

Desa Purwa is the old Geger Sunten, where Aki and Nini Balangantrang raised a foundling boy, **Ciung Wanara**. The false king of Galuh wants that boy dead. On the night of the village *wayang ruwatan*, his **Faceless** (*Pajang Teken*) and their commander, the **Nameless Hunter** (*Si Tanpa Ngaran*), raid the village for him. Ciung has already run west. In the crowd, your younger sister **Laras** (`La-Ra-Sa`) looks at the masked Hunter and whispers "Aria", a name nobody has said to him in years. Laras can hear True Names. The Hunter takes her instead: "Wrong child. A better one."

By morning nobody in Desa Purwa remembers Laras, not even Ibu. Only you, Semar and the fading letters on Laras's palm-leaf *lontar* still remember. You are a player-named hero; your lines use `@NPC_HERO_NAME`. Across six regions you follow Laras's trail. Meanwhile the Hunter uses her gift, and the war between Galuh and Mataram, to break the **Four Great Seals** and wake the **Eclipse of Batara Kala**.

The game explains every plot thread in-game: in talks, in story beats and in the codex.

## The Four Great Seals
The ancestors wrote four dangerous stories down so they would stay *finished*. A written ending binds its curse. Those four writings are the Great Seals, and together they keep Kala asleep under the land.
- A seal can only be **unread** by someone who hears True Names. The Faceless have no names, so they can't read it. That is why the Hunter needs Laras.
- When a seal breaks, its legend **replays** (the old curse walks again) and Kala eats what people forget.
- The hero never breaks a seal. You **end the story again**, the way the legend ended. That stops the rampage. Whatever Kala has already eaten stays eaten until Kala is calmed in the finale.

| # | Seal | Who breaks it, where | How you end it again |
|---|------|----------------------|----------------------|
| 1 | The Crater (Sangkuriang) | Laras is made to read it at Tangkuban Perahu. The Faceless Captain guards it until it cracks (Act I) | The false dawn: hang the *Boeh Rarang* on the eastern post and cast Ca |
| 2 | The Royal Name of Galuh | Barma Wijaya himself, years ago. He gave Kala his own name and scraped King Nagawiru's name off the Batu Tulis. This was the first crack and the reason the forgetting began | Write Nagawiru's true name (`Na-Ga`) on the Pamali stone (Act III). Caging the false king does not do it: "the cage holds the man, not the name" |
| 3 | The Thousand Temples (Bandung Bondowoso) | Laras is made to read it at the Hunter's camp in Candi Seribu (Act III) | The lesung women's false-dawn rhythm and Jawa Ca, as Roro Jonggrang's women did |
| 4 | The Keris (Mpu Gandring) | Laras is made to read it at the eastern shrine, and the Hunter draws the blade (Act IV) | Lay the keris to rest, then decide its fate (Choice 3) |

When the fourth seal opens, the Eclipse begins. The Eclipse eats first the name of whoever read the seals open.

Codex: the seals are a game invention built on the real legends. The codex marks each line "legend" or "game".

## Laras's trail
Laras isn't a sack on someone's back. She fights back, runs when she can, and keeps writing your name on her lontar so she won't lose it. Each act has one **trace** (something of hers you find) and one **Laras beat** (her voice in the dark at a petilasan, 2-3 lines).

| Act | Trace | Laras beat |
|-----|-------|------------|
| Prologue | Her lontar, left on the wayang stage | None (the prologue cards show her) |
| I Tatar Sunda | Her ribbon at the bamboo edge, where she broke free. Elder Abah Karna saw her with "a wild boy". In the river gorge at dusk, Sandekala cornered them and their tracks split. Her warning is carved under the Batu Tulis: "They need me to read." The Weaver hid her for a night, before the Faceless took her back on the terraces | `laras_1`, at the crater road: a man with no face keeps asking her to read; she reads slowly on purpose |
| II Keraton Galuh | Her message scratched on the cell wall: "The thousand temples. Don't stop." The Deserter guarded her and let her keep her lontar | `laras_2`, in the palace courtyard: they moved her in the night, east, under a wagon cloth |
| III Mataram | A strip of her lontar in the Candi Seribu camp, with your name written again and again | `laras_3`, at the temple petilasan: she read the seal because they hurt the prisoners when she didn't |
| IV Tanah Timur | **You see her**, across the eastern shrine, made to read the keris seal. When the Hunter falls, the Eclipse takes her | `laras_4`, at the desert petilasan: she can't remember your face, only one letter of your name |
| V Segara Kidul | Her voice comes off the water | `laras_kidul`, at the cliff shrine: she can hear the sea, and the sea still says her name |
| VI Gua Kala | The three syllables of her name, held by Kala as Memory Seals | `laras_5`, at the last petilasan: "Who's there?" She has forgotten you, but she is still writing |

## Acts (68 main steps, about 8 hours, TUNE)
**Prologue: Desa Purwa (MQ0, 8 steps).**
- The wayang night. The Faceless come for Ciung Wanara, but Laras whispers "Aria" and is taken.
- At dawn Ibu doesn't remember Laras. Semar does: he explains the Curse of Forgetting and teaches `Ka`.
- You drive a second Faceless raid out of the village square. At the west gate, the Hunter says the words that frame the whole game: "Wrong child. A better one."
- Nini Balangantrang tells you her Ciung ran west in the night, and the Faceless went the same way.

**Act I: Tatar Sunda, "The Crater Seal" (MQ1, 13 steps).**
- Learn `Ga`, `Nga`, `Ca` (Sunda).
- Follow Laras's trail: the ribbon, Abah Karna, the dusk fight with **Sandekala** in the river gorge, her warning under the defaced Batu Tulis, and the Weaver who hid her and gives you the *Boeh Rarang*. Queen Naganingrum by the lake senses her son and points you to the crater.
- At the crater road, Laras's voice. At the rim, defeat the **Faceless Captain**. With his last strength he smashes the cracked seal-stone, and **Sangkuriang** wakes (Seal 1).
- End the legend again with the false dawn. Free **Ciung Wanara**, chained there as bait, and the naga-king **Nagawiru**.
- Nagawiru: "The man on the throne wears my face. They took the girl to Galuh." The egg Ciung carried hatches. He wants his throne, and you want your sister. You go east together.

**Act II: Keraton Galuh, "The Borrowed Face" (MQG, 8 steps).** No new glyphs; rewards are gear and pusaka.
- Plan with Ciung at the square gates. Win the king's tournament (the rooster fights off-screen). Old chamberlain Uwa Lengser sees the queen's eyes in Ciung and opens the inner palace.
- Search the cells: Laras was here, but they moved her east the night before. Her message is on the wall.
- The **Faceless Deserter** guarded her. He tells you the Hunter is the usurper's own discarded name. **Choice 1:** free him or hand him to Lengser.
- Unmask **Prabu Barma Wijaya** in the throne room. Ciung cages him and Queen Pangrenyep, as in the legend.
- Barma Wijaya confesses Seal 2: he scraped the old king's name to keep the face, and "I only fed it." The Hunter, from the shadows: "The cage holds the man, not the name."
- Prince **Hariang Banga** escapes east to raise an army and free his mother.

**Act III: Mataram, "Blood on the Pamali" (MQ2, 13 steps).**
- Learn `Ha`, `Na`, `Ca`, `Ra` (Jawa) across the war-torn border, from Dalang Ki Sumarto, scout Pak Karto, the *gejog lesung* women and the juru kunci Mbah Marto.
- The Hunter's servants harvest the fallen in the graveyard. Two armies make twice the forgetting.
- Storm the Faceless camp at Candi Seribu and find Laras's lontar strip. She is gone east. **Bandung Bondowoso** wakes (Seal 3). End his night with the lesung rhythm and Jawa Ca.
- Write Nagawiru's name on the Pamali stone (Seal 2 restored). Duel Hariang Banga alone at the ford: you helped cage his mother, so it is a duel, not a battle.
- He yields. Nagawiru's voice from the water and Ciung Wanara on the far bank: fighting your brother is *pamali*. The land splits at the river, as in the legend. **Choice 2:** ask Ciung to show the caged king mercy, or say nothing.

**Act IV: Tanah Timur, "The Seven-Generation Blade" (MQ3, 11 steps).**
- Hariang Banga shows you the road east. Mpu Wirasana teaches you to break curse marks. Help Mbok Srini and defeat the curse-maddened **Buto Ijo** with Timun Mas's four pouches.
- Follow the Hunter's ash-trail down the volcanic coast and across the Sea of Sand.
- At the shrine you finally **see Laras**. She is made to read the last seal, and the **Keris Mpu Gandring** wakes (Seal 4). Lay it to rest. **Choice 3:** reforge it or give it to the river.
- Defeat **the Nameless Hunter** and carve his name, *Aria Kebonan*, back onto his split mask. In Galuh, the caged man gets his own face back.
- Then the Eclipse begins, and it takes Laras first. The Hunter, emptied: "She's gone where I was going."

**Act V: Segara Kidul, "The Sea Does Not Forget" (MQ4, 7 steps).**
- The mouth of Gua Kala is sealed by the Eclipse, a dark that eats glyphs. Semar: the sea does not forget, so go south.
- Juru kunci Mbah Wiryo teaches respect for the coast. Cross the reef and the misty grove, and hear Laras on the water at the cliff shrine.
- Pass Nyi Roro Kidul's **Trial of the Waves** and receive the ninth letter, `jawa_ka` *Ombak* (Tide), and the *Kain Hijau*.
- Ombak is the one letter the Eclipse can't eat. It opens the cave and breaks Kala's eclipse shield. (The sea remembering is a game invention.)

**Act VI: Gua Kala, "Into the Maw of Time" (MQ5, 8 steps).**
- Ciung Wanara and Hariang Banga hold the cave mouth side by side, one king from each bank. You go in alone.
- Reclaim the three Memory Seals, the syllables of her name (`La`, `Ra`, `Sa`), across the Maw, the Hourglass Sands and the Abyss of Forgetting.
- Rest with Semar at the last petilasan. Calm **Batara Kala** in the Ruwatan of Dawn with Sunda and Jawa letters in turn, and Ombak against the eclipse.
- Her lontar is blank. Semar: "Then write it." You draw `La-Ra-Sa` stroke by stroke, and she comes home.

**Epilogue.**
- 7 cards. Three of them change with your choices (`choice_freed`/`choice_handed`, `choice_mercy`/`choice_silent`, `choice_reforge`/`choice_sink`).
- Everything Kala ate comes back. Ibu pours two cups and nobody thinks Laras ever left. Only you remember the road.
- The village wayang plays your story. The credits show codex completion.

## Delivery
- NPC dialogue in short boxes, up to 6 lines per main-path talk (TUNE, was 4). The hero and the NPC take turns. Every talk ends on a lead, and lore goes to the codex.
- Story beats ([quests.md](quests.md) 4) carry the plot between steps, so there are no "return to Semar" steps.
- The wayang kulit prologue and epilogue use simple shadow-puppet silhouettes: cheap in pixel art and strongly cultural.
- Each zone has 3-5 NPCs. Some teach glyphs (main quest), others give side quests.
- All text is in Indonesian and English.

## Sensitivity
- **Cockfighting** is in the legend. The hero never bets or handles a rooster, and the bouts happen off-screen. The codex says the legend tells it and the game doesn't endorse it (**VERIFY** the law before any codex line).
- **Sunda and Java:** the legend's message is that brothers don't fight. Neither side is the bad side.
- **Kala and ruwatan:** Kala is calmed and turned back, never killed. The codex explains ruwatan as a living tradition.
- **Nyi Roro Kidul** is presented as belief, with respect, and is never fought.
- **The Majapahit claim** (Hariang Banga as founder) only ever appears as "some tellings say".

Main quest steps, 3-4 side quests per zone (folklore retellings, spirit bounties, errands), rewards and the quest system are in [quests.md](quests.md).

There is a single ending. Each of the 3 choices changes one epilogue card, and codex completion changes the credits text only (**ASSUMED**).
