# Aksara Kala showcase trailer: optional narration

The finished trailer (`AksaraKala_Showcase.mp4`, 4:14.6) has **no voice**. This is a script the team can record later and
lay over it. Timestamps are from the final timeline (`video/edit/timeline.md`). Each line fits its range at no more
than about 2.3 words per second, so a natural read lands inside it. Tone: a confident game-trailer narrator. Not a lecture.

Rules this script keeps:
- Nothing is spoken over the hook's burst (0:06.2-0:07.5), the lamp going out (0:18.9-0:20.4) or the intro's storm, Ka
  and logo slam (0:27.5-0:36.7). The music and game sound carry those moments.
- The cards already show their lines, so the narration leaves them alone. The quote cards (2:59.6-3:16.6) get only two
  short lines, placed so viewers can still read the quotes.
- Facts come only from `video/PLAN.md`. Batara Kala is calmed (ruwatan), never killed.
- When mixing a recorded voice in, duck the music 4-6 dB under each line (`video/edit/music_plan.py`, `DUCKS`).
